How to Play
1

Pick a mandala

Tap START, choose a design from the gallery, then press BEGIN. New designs unlock as you finish the ones before them.

2

Tap to color

Tap any shape to fill it with your selected color. Tap it again with a different color to change it.

3

The color bar

Along the bottom is your five-color palette plus the eraser (white swatch with the red ring). Swipe the bar to browse every available color — it loops around — and tap the ⇆ button to switch the bar between horizontal and vertical.

4

Make your palette

Pull the bar up to open the palette editor: tap a slot, then choose any swatch from All Colors to drop it in. Close it and your new palette is ready in the bar. (You can also edit it from COLOR PALETTE on the menu.)

5

Handy tools

Tap the gear on the right for extra tools: a brush to fill by dragging across shapes, an eyedropper to grab a color straight from your artwork, and a reset to start the mandala over.

6

Zoom, pan & undo

Pinch to zoom into the fine center detail and drag to move around. Tap the frame button to fit the whole mandala again, and the curved arrow to undo your last fill.

7

Save & personalize

Your work saves automatically on this device — tap the check mark to mark a mandala complete. In Settings you can switch between Light and Dark themes and set the music and sound volume. It all works fully offline, no internet ever.
