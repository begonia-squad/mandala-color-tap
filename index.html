How to Play
1

Pick a mandala

Tap START, choose any design from the gallery, then press BEGIN.

2

Choose a color

Select one of the five colors in the bar at the bottom of the coloring screen.

3

Tap to fill

Tap any shape to flood it with your color. Tap again with a new color to change it.

4

Zoom & pan

Pinch to zoom into the fine center detail, drag to move around, and tap the frame button to fit the whole mandala again.

5

Erase & undo

The white swatch with the red ring erases a shape. The curved arrow undoes your last fill.

6

Save your art

Your work saves automatically on this device. Tap the check mark to mark a mandala complete. No internet needed — ever.
